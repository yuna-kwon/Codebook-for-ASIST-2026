# ASIS&T Annual Meeting 2026 - Poster

Title: Archive, Continue, or Delete? Examining Account Management in Political Leaders’ Social Media

Authors: Yuna Kwon, Yi-Yun Cheng

## Abstract

As social media serves as an official communication channel for political leaders, preserving its digital records has become an important archival task. However, existing research on political social media archiving has primarily focused on challenges in collecting and preserving the content and metadata of presidential accounts, while paying little attention to account-level management that would ultimately affect whether the content remains accessible over time. To understand current account management practices, this study examines the management patterns of political leaders’ social media accounts using an exploratory account-level analysis. We developed a codebook to analyze 117 political leaders’ accounts from 29 countries in terms of access type, activity type, account type, and account succession pattern. The findings suggest that account management practices should be considered as part of social media archiving discussions, and that archiving efforts should include account management planning.
